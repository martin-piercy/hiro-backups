Privacy policy: hiro-backups

Last updated: 29 October 2026

  • Who uses it: one person, the owner of this site, for backing up their own server.
  • Google user data accessed: only files and folders this app creates in the owner's Google Drive, through the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope. It reads no other Drive content, and no profile, contacts, email or other Google data.
  • How it's used: to upload, verify, list and delete the owner's encrypted backup files. Nothing else.
  • Storage and sharing: backup files are encrypted on the server before upload. The OAuth token stays only on the owner's server. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or model training.
  • Revoking access: remove the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
